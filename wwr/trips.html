Our Exciting Trips
Trips Description
Los Dientes Experience the thrill of rafting through the Los Dientes canyon, with breathtaking scenery and exciting rapids suitable for all skill levels. This trip includes a professional guide, safety equipment, and a delicious riverside lunch. The journey covers approximately 12 miles of river, featuring both calm stretches and challenging Class III-IV rapids. Perfect for families, groups, and adventure seekers looking for an unforgettable day on the water.
Big Mallard Tackle the wild waters of Big Mallard, renowned for its exhilarating Class IV-V rapids and stunning mountain vistas. This trip is designed for experienced rafters seeking a challenging adventure. All participants receive expert instruction, top-quality gear, and support from our seasoned guides. The route spans 15 miles and includes a riverside picnic and photo opportunities at scenic overlooks.
A Half Day Adventure Enjoy a relaxing float down Laguna Verde, perfect for beginners and families. This gentle trip features calm waters, lush green surroundings, and opportunities for swimming and wildlife spotting. The excursion covers 8 miles and includes snacks, safety equipment, and friendly guides who share local stories and nature facts along the way.
